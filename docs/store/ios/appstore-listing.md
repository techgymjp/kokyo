```
朝の皇居。お濠沿いの道には、たくさんのランナーが走っている。
ロードバイクにまたがって、ランナーや歩行者、車やバイクをよけながら、皇居を一周（約6km）走り抜けろ！

■ 実在の東京を、そのまま走る

・左右、ブレーキ、ダッシュの4つだけ
```

```
Morning at the Imperial Palace in Tokyo. The road along the moat is packed with joggers.
Hop on your road bike and ride the full loop around the Palace (about 6 km), dodging runners, pedestrians, cars and motorbikes!

■ RIDE THE REAL TOKYO
• Buildings come from PLATEAU, Japan's official 3D city model. Hills follow real elevation data, and lane counts follow OpenStreetMap
• Start at Nijubashi-mae, ride past Otemon Gate, Takebashi, Kudanshita, Chidorigafuchi, Hanzomon Gate, Miyakezaka and Sakuradamon Gate, and finish back at Nijubashi-mae
• Crawl up Kudan-zaka, then fly down Miyakezaka at 80 km/h!

■ SQUEEZE THROUGH
• The moat-side lane is open to runners: solo joggers, pairs, trios, tired walkers, and the odd Edo-period hikyaku messenger
• Cars come at you in the oncoming lanes, and motorbikes split the lanes. Swing out for speed, if you dare
• You can ride on the sidewalk too, but Chidorigafuchi's cobblestones will shake you. Road edge or sidewalk?
• Hit anyone and it's game over. Just clip an edge and you only slow down: "Grazed!"

■ SIMPLE CONTROLS
• Left, right, brake and dash. That's it
• You pedal automatically up to 40 km/h. Dashing past 40 km/h uses stamina

■ SIGHTSEEING AT SPEED
• Nippon Budokan, Tokyo Station's Marunouchi Building, Otemon and Hirakawa-mon gates, Wadakura Fountain Park, Chidorigafuchi, Hanzomon Gate, the National Diet Building, Sakuradamon Gate and Nijubashi Bridge
• At each landmark the camera swings around to show it off

■ FOUR STAGES
• Morning, Day, Dusk and Night. Finish a stage to unlock the next, with more people and more traffic
• From Day on, an oni riding a cloud chases you. When it catches up, lightning strikes!
• Near Sakuradamon, Ii Naosuke's procession is crossing the road in the snow... find the gaps!

■ AND MORE
• Fireworks at the finish. Earn 1 to 3 stars for your time and beat your best
• Crashed? Watch an ad to keep riding from just before the crash (up to 5 times per game)
• Share your result with friends
• Plays offline (ads and sharing need a connection)
• English and Japanese

Ready? The race starts at Nijubashi-mae. Can you make it all the way around?

Buildings: MLIT Project PLATEAU / Elevation & water: GSI Japan / Roads: © OpenStreetMap contributors
```
